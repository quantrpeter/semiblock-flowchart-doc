# Exercise 1 — Making a Cup of Tea

## Task

Create a flowchart to show the steps of making a cup of tea.

Your flowchart should include:

- Start
- Boil water
- Put a tea bag into a cup
- Pour hot water into the cup
- Wait for the tea to brew
- Serve the tea
- End

## Requirement

Use:

- **Start/End**
- **Process**
- **Arrow**

## Challenge

Can you add more steps, such as adding milk or sugar?
